After downloading:

  • Double click app to launch. macOS will pop a scary alert. Click "Done"
  • Open System Settings
  • Go to Privacy & Security pane
  • Scroll to bottom.
  • Click "Open Anyway" next to "Antarctica was blocked"
  • Click "Open Anyway" on the alert that appears
  • Provide fingerprint or password
  • Read the User Manual!
  • Enjoy!
